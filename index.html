Happy Birthday,
Nautanki. 🌸

For all the years we've spent knowing each other, being far away, being close in some ways, and somehow still not knowing everything about each other in others, one thing I can say with certainty is that you became a genuinely important part of my life.

A lot changes with time. People change, circumstances change, and sometimes life takes people in completely different directions than they once imagined. But one thing that never really changed for me is that I've always wanted good things for you. I still do, genuinely and without expecting anything in return.

I hope this year brings you the kind of happiness that doesn't need to be loud to be meaningful. The kind that exists in peaceful mornings, good conversations, people who make you feel understood, and those random, stupid moments that make you laugh when you weren't even expecting to — and I genuinely, genuinely wish that, while writing this, it fills my heart too.

I hope the things that feel heavy right now become a little lighter with time. And I hope you have people around you who make life feel a little softer, especially on the days when you're tired and have too much on your mind.

And somewhere in all of this, I hope you remember to take care of yourself too. You've got a completely different kind of life now, with everything that comes with being a mom. The responsibility, the exhaustion, the constant giving, and probably not enough time to just sit and breathe. I hope that, somewhere in the middle of all that "auntie" business, you still get moments where you don't have to be responsible for anyone or anything. Moments where you can just be Komal. Not someone's mother, not someone's daughter, not the person taking care of everyone else — just you.

I also hope you've learned to be a little kinder to the person you were. We don't always make the right choices, and not everything works out the way we thought it would. But I think growing up is partly learning that you don't have to keep punishing yourself for things you've already learned from. Some chapters are better left with understanding rather than regret.

For everything that belongs to the past, I hope you can look back at it gently. Not necessarily wishing it were different, but being grateful for what it taught you and then letting it stay where it belongs.

For the present, I hope you find enough peace to actually enjoy your life while you're living it. It's very easy to spend so much time worrying about what's next that you forget you're already in a life you once knew nothing about.

And for everything that's still ahead, I genuinely hope life is kind to you. I hope you grow into someone you're proud of, find people who make you feel at home, have reasons to laugh more than you have reasons to worry, and build a life that feels like it truly belongs to you.

You're 27 now. It's such a strange age because you're old enough to understand yourself and life a little better, but still young enough that so much of it is yet to be figured out. You don't have to have everything sorted. You don't have to rush into becoming some perfect version of yourself. Just keep growing, keep learning, and keep choosing the things and people that make your life feel honest and yours.

Whatever life looks like from here, I hope it treats you well.

Have a beautiful birthday, Komal. I really mean every bit of this. 🌷
